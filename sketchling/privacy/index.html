Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Batoucada ("we", "us", or "our") built Sketchling as a free application. This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. Sketchling works entirely offline and does not collect your name, email address, location, device identifiers, photos, or any other personal information. There are no analytics, cookies, or advertising SDKs, and there is no account.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your favorite drawings and your starting level are stored locally on your device only. We have no access to this data, and it is never transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Printing and Sharing

When you print a page, Sketchling hands the drawing to your device's own print service, which you choose and control. When you share a page, the drawing is passed to the app you pick in the Android share sheet. In both cases only the coloring page itself is sent, and only because you asked; Sketchling does not see or keep what happens next.

Permissions

Sketchling requests no permissions. It does not need internet access: every coloring page is included in the app.

Third-Party Services

Sketchling does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or libraries that collect user data.

Children's Privacy

Sketchling is designed for children and families. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, there is nothing to disclose, delete, or manage. The only link that leaves the app, to this privacy policy, sits behind a simple question for grown-ups.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at contact@batoucada.fr.
