ONDARA

Control your Sony speakers and soundbars over Wi-Fi.

Ondara finds Sony network audio devices on the Wi-Fi network your phone is already on and talks to them directly, phone to speaker — no cloud service, no account, no sign-in.

Get it on Google Play → Privacy

What it does

Every Sony model exposes a different set of features, so Ondara asks each device what it supports and shows only that.

🔍

Finds your devices

A fast pass listens for the devices that announce themselves, then a bounded, cancellable check of the addresses on your own subnet catches the ones that do not. Whatever it finds is remembered, so the next launch opens straight onto your speakers.

🎛️

Only the controls your device has

Ondara reads each device’s own description of itself before drawing anything. A control the speaker in front of you cannot obey is not shown at all — not greyed out, not present.

🔊

Volume from the device’s own range

Minimum, maximum and step come from the device itself rather than a guessed 0–50, so the slider means what it says. Mute and power on / back to standby sit alongside it.

🎚️

Sound field and equaliser

Sound field, DSEE HX and ClearAudio+ where the device offers them, listed in the device’s own words, plus the 5-band equaliser when it is on its custom sound field.

🔋

Battery, only when it is real

Portable speakers that report a level get one. Devices that do not get no battery element at all — and a level Ondara cannot read is shown as unknown, never as a guessed number.

🏠

Sees your speaker groups

If your devices are in a Google Cast group, Ondara shows the group, its members and which device is currently leading it. Creating and editing groups stays in Google Home — Ondara only reads.

Compatibility

Ondara is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sony.

Supported devices
Sony wireless speakers and soundbars that answer on the local network. Development devices are the SRS-HG1 speaker and the HT-XT3 soundbar; not every model answers, and firmware makes a difference, so run a scan before deciding
Android version
8.0 (API 26) and above
Network
Your phone and your speaker must be on the same Wi-Fi network. This cannot work over mobile data, and Wi-Fi hardware is declared as required
Hardware needed
A Sony network audio device — the app does nothing on its own. With none on the network it opens normally and reports that it found nothing
Permissions
INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE to reach the device on your own network, plus CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE on Android 13 and below for the multicast lock discovery needs. No location permission of any kind
Sony’s own app
Not required to use Ondara, and Ondara is not a replacement for it

⚠️ What to expect

The interface these devices expose is undocumented; Ondara was built by observing the devices themselves. So it never assumes: every control is derived from what that particular device declares about itself, and a device that declares nothing is asked for nothing. On a model we have not seen, a command can still be refused — Ondara reports that rather than pretending it worked.

These devices also have no authentication of any kind. Any app or computer on the same network can already change their volume or switch them off; that is a property of the hardware, not something Ondara introduces or can fix. Ondara is provided as is, without warranty.
